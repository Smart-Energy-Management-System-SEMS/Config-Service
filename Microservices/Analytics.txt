### Resumen de bodies esperados

- `CreateDeviceIdentificationRequest`: `user_id, device_id, average_daily_kwh?, predicted_device_type?, confidence_score?, status?`
- `CreateBillPredictionRequest`: `user_id, prediction_year, prediction_month, period_start, period_end, historical_consumption_kwh[], tariff_per_kwh?, currency?, estimated_kwh?, estimated_amount?, error_margin_percentage?`
- `CreateRecommendationRequest`: `user_id, device_id?, recommendation_type?, current_kwh?, average_kwh?, estimated_saving_kwh?, tariff_per_kwh?, currency?`
- `CreateAnomalyRequest`: `user_id, device_id, actual_kwh, expected_kwh?, historical_kwh[]?, threshold_percentage?, anomaly_type?, description?`
- `CreateConsumptionRankingRequest`: `user_id, period_type, period_start, period_end, devices[{device_id, device_name, total_kwh}], tariff_per_kwh?, currency?`

## 4) Integración con Kafka

### Topics que consume

- `energy.consumption.recorded`
- `device.registered`
- `device.updated`

### Topics que produce

- `analytics.bill_prediction.generated`
- `analytics.recommendation.generated`
- `analytics.anomaly.detected`
- `analytics.device_identified`
- `analytics.consumption_ranking.generated`

### Eventos escuchados y payload esperado

- `device.registered` / `device.updated`
  - Requeridos: `user_id`, `device_id`
  - Opcional: `average_daily_kwh`
- `energy.consumption.recorded`
  - Requeridos: `user_id`, `device_id`, `actual_kwh`
  - Opcionales: `expected_kwh`, `historical_kwh[]`

### Eventos publicados y payload

Se publica la entidad persistida completa (incluye `id` y timestamps).  
Ejemplos de shape:
- `analytics.device_identified`: `id, user_id, device_id, predicted_device_type, confidence_score, status, analyzed_at, created_at`
- `analytics.bill_prediction.generated`: `id, user_id, prediction_year, prediction_month, period_start, period_end, estimated_kwh, estimated_amount, currency, tariff_used, error_margin_percentage, generated_at, created_at`
- `analytics.recommendation.generated`: `id, user_id, device_id, recommendation_type, title, description, estimated_saving_kwh, estimated_saving_amount, currency, status, generated_at, applied_at, created_at`
- `analytics.anomaly.detected`: `id, user_id, device_id, anomaly_type, description, severity, status, actual_kwh, expected_kwh, deviation_percentage, detected_at, resolved_at, created_at`
- `analytics.consumption_ranking.generated`: `id, user_id, period_type, period_start, period_end, rankings[], generated_at, created_at`
