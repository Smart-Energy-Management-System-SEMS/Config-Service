### 3) Reglas de seguridad

Estado actual en código:
- **JWT:** no implementado en el microservicio.
- **Roles:** no implementados.

Recomendación para Gateway:
- **Públicos:**
  - `GET /api/v1/payments/health`
  - `POST /api/v1/payments/webhooks/stripe` (validar `Stripe-Signature`)
- **Protegidos con JWT:**
  - Todos los demás endpoints de negocio (`payment-methods`, `payments`, `invoices`)

Headers:
- `Authorization: Bearer <token>` (en rutas protegidas por gateway)
- `Content-Type: application/json` (endpoints JSON)
- `Stripe-Signature: <firma>` (webhook Stripe)

### 4) Integración Kafka

**Topics que produce** (default):
- `payment.processed`
- `payment.failed`
- `invoice.generated`
- `payment.method.added`

**Topics que consume** (default):
- `subscription.created`
- `subscription.renewal.requested`
- `subscription.cancelled`

**Eventos publicados + payload:**
- `payment.processed`: `event_type, occurred_at, payment_id, subscription_id, user_id, amount, currency, status, invoice_id`
- `payment.failed`: `event_type, occurred_at, payment_id, subscription_id, user_id, amount, currency, status`
- `invoice.generated`: `event_type, occurred_at, invoice_id, payment_id, invoice_number, issued_at, total_amount, pdf_url`
- `payment.method.added`: `event_type, occurred_at, payment_method_id, user_id, type, brand, last4, is_default`

**Eventos escuchados + payload mínimo:**
- `subscription.created`: `subscription_id, user_id`
- `subscription.renewal.requested`: `subscription_id, user_id, payment_method_id, amount` (`currency` recomendado)
- `subscription.cancelled`: `subscription_id, user_id` (`reason` opcional)
